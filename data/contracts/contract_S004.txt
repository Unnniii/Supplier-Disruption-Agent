FRAME AGREEMENT NG-2021-063
Northgate Motors Ltd and Lviv Harness Systems (S004), Lviv, Ukraine.
Products: main body harness, door harness, engine bay harness and battery pack harness.

Article 10 Force majeure, including war, civil unrest and government restrictions. Notice within 3 days. If supply is interrupted for more than 30 days the Buyer may re-source.

Article 12 Dual sourcing. Wroclaw Wiring Sp z o.o. (S014) is approved as a second source for door harness and engine bay harness only. Main body harness and battery pack harness are single sourced from Lviv; new tooling for an alternate would take 10 to 14 weeks.

Article 14 The Supplier keeps 10 days of finished harness stock in a bonded warehouse in Rzeszow, Poland.
